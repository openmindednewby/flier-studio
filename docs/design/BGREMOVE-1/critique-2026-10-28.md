## Findings for the redo (not a score)

1. **Matte at the fingertips fails the AC-4 proof** (`fingertips-8x.png`). A 2-6 px band of grey-brown gravel stays under
   all four fingertips and in the web between fingers, and a gold chunk of stone (~10x15 px at 1x) stays left of the index
   finger. On the green check it reads as dirt, not shadow. D3 moved the fingertip proof into AC-4; this capture is
   the model output without the brush or a brushed result, and in either case it is not clean. Fix: erase-brush the strip and the gold
   chunk, then recapture at 8x on the same green; the band must be gone, with the finger edge kept (no bite into the nails).
2. **"Remove background" does not say which image it acts on.** It sits under both Upload picture and Upload logo as an
   equal-weight full-width outline, so at 360 it reads as acting on the logo as much as the picture. Fix: label it
   "Remove picture background", or put it in the picture column at half width under Upload picture with the logo cell left
   empty. Either way the button stays (no fix by removal).
3. **The dialog and the 4 PNGs are not shown.** AC-4 asks for critic PASS on the 4 re-cut Golden hour PNGs, and the brief
   names the keep/erase dialog; neither is captured. Fix: capture the dialog at 360x640 and 1280x800 in the cutting, cut and
   brushed states (FOCUS-1: Cancel focused while cutting, Use cut-out after), plus the 4 Golden hour fliers.

Rules observed on the entry row: R6 controls radius 8 matches its siblings; R8 outlined, not a second filled primary (Download
all PNGs stays the only filled one); R1 values unchanged; R3 saturation unchanged (no accent added); R11 not touched by
this change. Text fits at both viewports; no AI-default tells.

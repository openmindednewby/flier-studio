   finger. On the green check it reads as dirt, not shadow. D3 moved the fingertip proof into AC-4; this capture is

   "Remove picture background", or put it in the picture column at half width under Upload picture with the logo cell left
   empty. Either way the button stays (no fix by removal).
3. **The dialog and the 4 PNGs are not shown.** AC-4 asks for critic PASS on the 4 re-cut Golden hour PNGs, and the brief
   names the keep/erase dialog; neither is captured. Fix: capture the dialog at 360x640 and 1280x800 in the cutting, cut and
   brushed states (FOCUS-1: Cancel focused while cutting, Use cut-out after), plus the 4 Golden hour fliers.

## Hard fails (checked first)

### 1. Lost item: none in A, B or C
Measured from `frames/*.json` `visibleItems` against the 63 rows: every row the live build shows on first load (52) is visible in every option's frames. The two missing ids are `theme-row` (container, replaced by `all-fliers`, its children `pick-theme` + `pick-scope` drawn there) and `pick-theme-scope` (dropped by D11 "AC-18..20 approved", theme is global only). The other 11 rows are state-only (lead, status, prompt-status, selected, list-hint, invalid, render-pending, mini-sheet, import-dialog, cutout-dialog, dark) and are not on the before frames either; A and C draw `mini-sheet` as the enlarge view.

**Count on the page is wrong.** The page types `data-inventory="63/63"`; `result.json` says `52/52`; the picture shows 52 of 52 first-load items kept plus 1 dropped by decision. `check-before-after.mjs` fails a page whose typed count differs from its `result.json`. Whatever the next round is, the page must carry the measured count (52/52, with the 11 state-only rows and the D11 drop named in the inventory note), not 63/63.

1. Move "Drafts on this device" out of the All fliers card to a card under the preview grid (C's placement), keeping it open with all three rows; the All fliers card then holds only Theme, Shared text edits and Add flier (~150 px) and the first flier starts near y = 400 at 1280.

**B** (hard fail, stays REDO until fixed):

Put "Add flier" and "Download all PNGs" on one shared height at 360: either stack them full width (one per row) below 400 px, or shorten the label to "Download all" so both stay one line and 44 px tall; build the row with `InlineFieldRow` and verify with `expectFieldsAligned` (top and height, not only bottom).

3. Fix the page count to 52/52 as above.

**C** (hard fail, stays REDO until fixed):

2. Drafts strip at 360: let it scroll horizontally inside the card with the third tile fully inside the padding, or show two tiles per row; no clipped "Today 15:20" or "Restore".

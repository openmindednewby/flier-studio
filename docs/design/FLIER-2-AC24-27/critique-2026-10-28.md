## Top fixes

**C (PASS, fixes optional before the pick):**
1. Put a one-line "Generate with on-device AI" entry (collapsed Generate header, 44 px, with the keyword-fallback note on open) above the fliers in the right column, so AC-24's AI claim is on the first screen at 1280 and 360.
2. Drop the "ON THIS PAGE" label from the contents card; the numbered links already say what it is.
3. Add "on-device AI" to the lead line ("Describe it and on-device AI drafts the flier, or fill the form ...") so the lead stops describing only the form.

**A (REDO):**
1. Move the rail into the top of the left column at 1280 (drop the third zone), so the right column is wide enough for three fliers per row again and the fliers start above y 300.
2. At 360 lay the six links as a 2x3 grid (as C does) instead of the 3 / 2 / 1 wrap; saves ~70 px.
3. Shrink Download all PNGs to its content width on the header row at 1280.

**B (REDO):**
1. Give every section `scroll-margin-top` equal to the bar height so a jump (and the drafts anchor) lands with its heading below the bar.
2. Fit all six pills at 1280 (shorter labels or two rows) so "Saved configs" is visible without an in-bar scroll.
3. At 360 end the pill row with a visible fade or wrap to two rows instead of cutting a pill in half.

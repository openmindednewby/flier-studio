## Top fixes

**A "Everything on the row"**
1. At 360 give the 4 row buttons an 8 px gap with no overlap (or take B's star icon + "More" menu for Rename/Delete) so Starred and Rename never share a border.
2. Per-row Delete: neutral 1 px outline with a 16 px trash glyph; red only on the confirm button and inside the Undo toast.
3. Toast: bottom offset = mini-preview bar height + 8 px at 360, so the draft name and the Undo button are fully visible for the 5 s.

**B "One menu per row"**
1. At 360 put the name + meta on their own full-width line above the Restore / star / More row (A's row shape), so no name breaks mid-word; at 1280 keep today's single row.
2. Toast: bottom offset = mini-preview bar height + 8 px at 360; recapture `toast` at 360 with Undo in frame.
3. Recapture `rename` and `cleanup` at 360 after fix 1 and show the dark `drafts` 360 frame on the page (only 1280 dark drafts is shown).

**C "Edit in place, select to clean up"**
1. Give the line tool a tap path: a "Line sizes" button in the Text group (or tap a line, then tap the next) that opens Same width / Same size / Reset; shift-click stays a desktop shortcut.
2. Dock the line toolbar under the flier card (beside Edit flier 1), never over the picture; mark the two picked lines with crop-mark corners (R6), not a rectangle.
3. Names wrap to two lines instead of clipping, and Select mode gets a visible "Delete all except favourites" action that opens the confirm with "Keep them" focused; toast above the bottom bar.

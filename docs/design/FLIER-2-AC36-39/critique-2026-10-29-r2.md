## Top fixes

**A "Everything on the row"**
1. Panel title from the field label only: "Style: Second title" (drop the button text from the string).
2. At 360 put Font on its own full-width row and Size (- 100% +) on the next, the value in a fixed 64 px cell between the steppers, so neither clips.
3. Keep the 2 x 2 row grid at 360, but at 1280 move Rename + Delete into one "More" button so a row carries 2 labelled buttons, not 4 (D13).

**B "One menu per row"**
1. Panel title from the field label only: "Style: Second title".
2. At 1280 open the style card below the input (top edge = input bottom + 8 px), never over it, so the text being styled stays visible.
3. At 360 the bottom sheet sits above the mini-preview bar (or hides the bar while open), with its own scroll and a Done button pinned at the bottom; recapture `style` at 360 with Alignment and Reset to theme in frame.

**C "Edit in place, select to clean up"**
1. Panel title from the field label only, and at 1280 give the docked panel the full right column width (all fliers move down while it is open), so the font select, letter spacing and "100%" fit uncut.
2. At 360 in Select mode pin "Delete all except favourites" above the mini-preview bar (sticky, bar height + 8 px), and recapture `cleanup` and `confirm` with one checkbox column and no text selection on the names.
3. When the panel opens at 1280, scroll so flier 1 and its corner marks stay in view beside the panel.

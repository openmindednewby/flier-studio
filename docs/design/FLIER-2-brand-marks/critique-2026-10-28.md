## Cross-cutting finding (all three)

**The wordmark is live system text, so it is not one mark.** Segoe UI (A, B) and Consolas (C) exist on Windows only. On macOS, iOS and Android the header and landing wordmark fall back to a different face, so R11 "one mark on all owned surfaces" fails on most phones. The OG and PNG icons are rasterised and stay correct; the HTML header does not. Every candidate needs the wordmark outlined to SVG paths (or one self-hosted face) before it ships.

## A "Trim": square with offset trim ticks

| # | Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Focal point / purpose | 4 | Solid square reads first; ticks say "print" at 32 px and up |
| 2 | Portability | 2 | At 16 and 32 px a square with ticks on all four sides is the stock microchip / "stop" / Figma frame-tool glyph |
| 3 | Hierarchy, type, colour | 3 | "**Flier** Studio" bold + light Segoe is a default system lockup; mark-to-text gap good |
| 4 | Visual language | 4 | Real offset crop marks (print-shop world), ink on paper, 0% saturated, ΔL* 80.7 grey/CVD |
| 5 | Taste | 4 | Calm, quiet; nothing rejected repeated |
| 6 | No AI tells | 4 | Clean |
| 7 | All sizes | 3 | fav16: ticks collapse to 1 px dashes, 40.8% edge density, sparkles; fine at 192/512, 50% width in safe zone |
| 8 | Inventory | 5 | 7/7 |

**Mean 3.6, lowest #2 = 2 → REDO.**
Top fixes:
1. Drop to two diagonal corner marks (top-left + bottom-right only) so the 16 px silhouette stops reading as a chip; keep the square.
2. Make the square portrait 3:4 (a flier sheet), not near-square, so the shape is paper, not a button.
3. 16 px hint: ticks 2 px thick, 3 px long, or omit them below 24 px and ship a separate 16 px glyph.

3. Scale the PWA 512/192 mark to ~55% of the canvas width (still inside the 80% safe circle).

## C "Register": sheet with a lifted corner and a registration target

| # | Criterion | Score | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Focal point / purpose | 4 | Registration target draws the eye; sheet + target reads "print" at 192 and up |
| 2 | Portability | 4 | Most specific to the approved print-shop world; no competitor glyph comes to mind |
| 3 | Hierarchy, type, colour | 2 | Letterspaced Consolas caps "FLIER STUDIO" reads as a developer tool / terminal, not a print shop; Consolas is Windows-only (see cross-cutting) |
| 4 | Visual language | 4 | Ink on paper, 0% saturated, ΔL* 80.7; the curl notch adds a second shape idea |
| 5 | Taste | 3 | Calm enough; the code-face voice is off-brief |
| 6 | No AI tells | 4 | Clean |
| 7 | All sizes | 2 | fav16: 38.3% ink, the ring and cross merge into a pixel blob and the notch reads as noise; the target is unreadable at the most-seen size |
| 8 | Inventory | 5 | 7/7 |

**Mean 3.5, lowest #3/#7 = 2 → REDO.**
Top fixes:
1. Replace the monospace wordmark with the same outlined sans as the app (normal tracking, mixed case).
2. Remove the lifted-corner notch; let the target overlap the sheet corner cleanly (one idea: sheet + register mark).
3. Ship a dedicated 16 px glyph: target as a 2 px cross only (no ring), or the sheet alone with a 2 px corner cross.

## Why REDO

The defect was "Generat / e" at 360. **Neither capture shows it.** The before capture already shows "Generate" on one line, because Flier is the active item. The likely trigger is `.rail a[aria-current="true"] { font-weight: 600 }`: a bold "Generate" is wider than the regular one, and `overflow-wrap: anywhere` then broke it mid-word. Both captures render Generate in regular weight, so they cannot show whether the break is gone. The pair passes parity but does not prove the fix. TEXT-FIT-1 "UI text never overflows its box at 360x640 or desktop, in every state" covers the active state too.

## Consistency note (same before and after, not caused by this patch)

"Saved configs" wraps to two lines at a word break, which is allowed. Its bookmark icon sits about 4 CSS px higher than the other five icons, because the rail item centres icon and caption as one block (`justify-content: center`). Read across, the icon row is uneven.

## Top fixes (max 3)

1. Capture both builds again at 360x640 with **Generate active** (bold), plus one with **Saved configs active**, using the same steps and the same scroll anchor for each build. Make the after capture show "Generate" on one line in bold.
2. Add the 1280x800 capture (vertical rail, 88px wide, labels at 0.72rem with bold active) and one dark-mode capture at 360.
3. Align the icon row: set `justify-content: flex-start` on `.rail a` below 1024px, so all six icons share one top edge and "Saved configs" wraps downward only.
